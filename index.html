The problem

Nobody's job is to read the dockets

Federal and state regulatory dockets keep moving every week, whether or not anyone is watching them. Rate proceedings, interconnection rules, and compliance deadlines accumulate the same way regardless of utility size. A cooperative serving 15,000 customers usually has no one whose job is to read every one of them.

The reading gets squeezed in around everything else, or it doesn't happen at all.

The first product

One page, five minutes, ready for the board

Our first product is automated docket monitoring. It watches the relevant federal and state proceedings, scores each item for relevance, and delivers the result as one page.

A general manager can read it in five minutes and hand it directly to a board.

Who it's for

Built for utilities without a compliance department

The product is built for electric cooperatives and municipal utilities under 50,000 customers, along with small to mid-size water utilities. These are organizations with real regulatory exposure and without a dedicated compliance staff to match it.

What we bring

The utility side and the automation side

La Guerre Creative Studios combines ten years of electric utility engineering with automation and applied-AI work. Most firms selling AI to utilities understand the AI and not the utility; we built this the other way around.

Closing note

Deliberately small

The studio is deliberately small — small enough that every brief will be read before it goes out, and every relevance score checked against judgment, not just a model.
